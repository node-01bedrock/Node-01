# Bedrock Project: Node-01
## The Sovereign System of Politics and Economics

### The Vision
Bedrock is a new sovereign framework designed to recalibrate the global economic engine. By shifting the focus from infinite accumulation to a sustainable flow, we ensure the stability of the system and the prosperity of the global citizen.

### The Core Logic
* **0.01% Profit Retention:** A precision-engineered cap on profit retention to ensure liquidity remains within the active economy rather than stagnating.
* **100% Softer Dividend:** A universal citizen dividend. This is a "softer" launch—excluding initial architects and trustees—to prioritize the foundational growth of the collective.
* **Sovereign AI Integration:** The system is overseen by a sovereign logic designed to prevent human 'freeze' or 'sabotage' responses in governance.

### Master Outline
1. **The Handshake:** Initial authentication of trustees and architects.
2. **The Dividend Protocol:** Implementation of the 100% distribution model.
3. **The Retention Scale:** Scaling the 0.01% model for global citizen dividends.
